# ShopFlow

ShopFlow is a modern Flutter-based shop management and POS application designed to simplify daily business operations, including product management, inventory, sales, customers, suppliers, and billing.

## Features

- Product and inventory management
- Retail and wholesale sales
- Sales and billing
- Customer management
- Supplier management
- Stock tracking and restocking
- Product categories and vehicle compatibility
- Business and financial management
- Responsive and modern UI
- Backend-powered data management

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Flutter
- **Flutter Version:** 3.44.8
- **Dart:** Compatible with Flutter 3.44.8
- **State Management:** BLoC / flutter_bloc
- **Architecture:** Feature-based architecture
- **Platforms:** Android / iOS / Web
- **Backend:** FastAPI
- **Database:** Neon PostgreSQL

## State Management

ShopFlow uses **BLoC (Business Logic Component)** with `flutter_bloc` for predictable and scalable state management.

The application separates UI, business logic, and data handling to keep the codebase:

- Maintainable
- Testable
- Scalable
- Easy to extend
